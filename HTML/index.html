About the Conference

Coding Conf brings together people who love building for the web. Whether you are writing your first line of HTML or shipping production applications, you will leave with new ideas, new skills and new friends.

Expect hands-on sessions covering semantic HTML, modern CSS layout, accessible design and JavaScript fundamentals, plus a lightning-talk stage where attendees share their own projects.
